Privacy Policy

This policy describes how ZEDTEX collects, uses, and protects information when you visit www.zedtex.us, contact us, or apply for a role. Last updated August 30, 2026.

ZEDTEX (“we,” “us”) operates the website at www.zedtex.us (the “Site”). We are a technology and financial consulting firm. This policy is intended to meet Google Ads landing-page and privacy-disclosure requirements as well as ordinary website use.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, optional phone number, and message. Applications sent to career@zedtex.us include whatever you attach (resume, LinkedIn URL, and related details).
  • Usage data. We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-9G9MPFSMP6) to understand how the Site is used — pages viewed, approximate location, device type, and referral source. Google may set cookies or similar identifiers as described in Google’s own policies.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider (Amazon S3 and CloudFront) may log IP address, user agent, and request time for security and delivery of the Site.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, evaluate job applications, operate and improve the Site, measure marketing (including Google Ads, if linked to our Analytics property), and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and advertising

Analytics cookies help us see which pages are useful. If we run Google Ads, conversion and remarketing tags may use cookies to measure ad performance. You can control cookies in your browser. Blocking cookies may limit Analytics and ads measurement; it does not block you from reading the Site.

Sharing

We share information with service providers who host the Site (Amazon Web Services), measure traffic (Google Analytics / Google), and deliver email when you contact us. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users.

Retention and security

Inquiry and application emails are retained as needed to respond and, for hiring, as long as the recruiting process requires. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings. We use HTTPS on www.zedtex.us. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold by emailing info@zedtex.us. You can opt out of Google Analytics using Google’s browser add-on, and manage ad personalization in your Google account.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@zedtex.us. Careers: career@zedtex.us.
