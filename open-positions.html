Senior Software Engineer

Full-Time 5+ Years Experience
Apply Now

We are seeking a highly skilled Senior Software Engineer with at least 5+ years of experience to join our dynamic software development team. This role requires a versatile, adaptable professional who embraces the entire software development lifecycle — from initial design to deployment and maintenance — and who excels in system design and performance optimization within an Agile environment.

  • Design and implement scalable, high-performance software systems according to business and technical requirements.
  • Participate actively in sprint planning sessions, ensuring accurate estimation and timely delivery of user stories and tasks.
  • Conduct rigorous code reviews to maintain code quality, security, and adherence to established best practices.
  • Optimize software performance through profiling, analysis, and refactoring to improve responsiveness and efficiency.
  • Promote and uphold Agile methodologies, encouraging continuous integration and continuous deployment practices.
  • Stay current with emerging technologies and industry trends to evaluate and integrate relevant tools and approaches.
  • Identify and resolve technical challenges proactively, ensuring minimal disruption to project timelines.
  • Document system designs, architecture decisions, and coding standards to facilitate team communication and onboarding.

Software Engineer

Full-Time
Apply Now

ZEDTEX is seeking a dedicated Software Engineer to contribute to the design, development, testing, and maintenance of high-quality software solutions. This role emphasizes development best practices — Agile methodology, test-driven development, and code review — and is a strong fit for a proactive, collaborative engineer who wants to grow across the full software development lifecycle.

  • Develop, test, and maintain reliable repositories following the full development lifecycle.
  • Implement test-driven development practices to ensure code quality and reduce defects.
  • Participate in code reviews to uphold coding standards, identify issues, and share knowledge with peers.
  • Collaborate with product managers, designers, and other engineers to deliver features according to requirements.
  • Apply Agile methodology principles by actively engaging in sprint planning, daily stand-ups, and retrospectives.
  • Document software design, code changes, and processes to maintain clear technical records.
  • Adhere to company policies, security standards, and quality assurance processes throughout the development cycle.

Outreach Executive

Remote Night Shift (US CST) Entry-Level
Apply Now

ZEDTEX is seeking energetic, motivated fresh graduates to join as Remote Outreach Executives. In this role, you'll proactively connect with US-based businesses, build strong relationships, and guide them through onboarding — a great launchpad for a career in international sales. The role runs night shifts aligned with US Central Standard Time, and pairs a fixed communication stipend with milestone-based compensation and uncapped commission.

  • Initiate outbound calls and messages to US-based businesses to generate new business leads.
  • Build authentic relationships with prospective clients by understanding their needs and offering tailored onboarding assistance.
  • Guide interested businesses through all stages of the onboarding process to ensure a smooth, positive experience.
  • Maintain organized, accurate records of all lead interactions, managing the pipeline from initial contact through final sign-up in the CRM.
  • Coordinate outreach activities during night shifts to align with US Central Standard Time and maximize engagement.
  • Utilize CRM tools, including HubSpot, to track progress, update contact information, and monitor lead status.
  • Collaborate with internal teams to provide feedback on customer needs and improve engagement and onboarding workflows.
  • Participate in training sessions to stay knowledgeable about the US market and enhance sales technique.
